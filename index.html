How Polaris Works

Polaris is our engineering AI agent — the cognitive layer that orchestrates analysis, compliance, and optimization so your engineers can focus on decisions that matter.

Step 1

Structural Geometry

Import IFC or pull from organizational context (RAG).

Step 2

Load Analysis

Imposed, wind and per-zone loads, cascaded top-down through the structure.

Step 3

FEA Simulation

Cloud-based finite element analysis on all structural elements in minutes.

Step 4

Compliance Check

Per-element checks flagging under-designed, over-designed and deflection failures.

Step 5

Optimization

Size, material and other changes, converging on safety, deflection and more.

Step 6

Calculation Reports

PDF, XLSX and IFC exports — load cases, utilization ratios, code references.

Steps 3–5 run in an iterative optimization loop until targets are met
